EDITORIAL CHARTER

Four rules of a trustworthy reference work.

Quranically is an indexed knowledge system, not an automatic answer engine or a dispensary of guaranteed cures.

01

Start with the source

Identify chapter, verse, collection and narrated reference before making an assertion.

02

Distinguish the layers

Revelation, transmission, scholarly interpretation, history and clinical evidence are different kinds of claims.

03

Show editorial status

Unreviewed material remains visibly unreviewed, even when its primary source can be located.

04

Keep commerce separate

Only independently screened ingredient offers may be listed. Retail products do not inherit religious or medical approval.
