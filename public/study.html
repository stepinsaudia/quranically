03Prophetic spiritual guidance and wellbeing

What can help the heart?

Authentic supplication and remembrance can be important sources of spiritual support. Depression and anxiety disorders also deserve appropriate assessment and evidence-based healthcare.

A verified supplication of Rasulullah ﷺ · Sahih al-Bukhari 6369
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْجُبْنِ وَالْبُخْلِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ، وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ
Allāhumma innī aʿūdhu bika mina l-hammi wa-l-ḥazani, wa-l-ʿajzi wa-l-kasali, wa-l-jubni wa-l-bukhli, wa-ḍalaʿi d-dayni, wa-ghalabati r-rijāl.
Meaning: O Allah, I seek Your protection from worry and grief, inability and inactivity, cowardice and miserliness, the burden of debt, and being overpowered by people.
Computer voice only · not an authenticated Hadith recitation
Reported from Anas ibn Malik · Sahih al-Bukhari, 6369
Spiritually grounded

Dhikr, Dua, prayer and connection

Quran 13:28 connects remembrance of Allah with reassurance of the heart. A believer may use remembrance, prayer and support from trusted people as spiritual practices without assuming immediate symptom relief.

Clinically responsible

Recognise when additional help is needed

Persistent low mood, panic, sleep disturbance or loss of functioning may merit assessment by a mental-health professional. Effective treatments exist, including psychological therapies and, when appropriate, medication.

Important distinction. “Tibb al-Nabawi” does not mean an authenticated prophetic cure has been established for clinical depression or anxiety. We should not prescribe herbs, cupping, Ruqyah or a number of recitations as a guaranteed treatment. If someone feels unsafe or at risk of self-harm, immediate local emergency or crisis support takes priority.
Religious evidence: Sahih al-Bukhari 6369; Quran 13:28. Clinical evidence: World Health Organization, “Depressive disorder (depression),” updated 2026. WHO describes effective treatment options. These are different kinds of evidence. Verify clinical source ↗
